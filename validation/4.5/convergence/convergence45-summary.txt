EightMax 4.5 convergence study of candidate trainer options (tools/convergence44.js with --only-extra)
10 seeds, six games, exact best response of every seat after each checkpoint; paired comparisons with the default
at equal sampled deals and at equal training time; Student t, Holm-adjusted over all 288 comparisons (family-wise 5%).
Two-player games: exploitability = NashConv / 2 is exact for the abstraction. All other games: NashConv is a sum of
unilateral best-response gains inside the abstraction, NOT exploitability. Values are mean +/- 95% CI over seeds.
B = variant supported better, W = supported worse, - = no supported difference (one letter per checkpoint).

Verdict: no candidate is supported better on every game or on a majority of comparisons. Latin-hypercube chance
sampling (alone or with the terminal baseline) has no supported difference anywhere; opponentExploration 0 stalls on
several games (unexplored actions are never revisited); 0.05 is supported better only in a few final-table checkpoints.
The validated default trainer is kept; the options stay opt-in and experimental. The study ran while other builds and
tests used the machine: equal-sample results are unaffected, equal-time results carry that extra noise.
