Study Mode work (training) runs separately, in its own window. Having a strategy is not proof of GTO accuracy: every stored strategy says "accuracy not proven" unless a measurement shows otherwise.

    What the messages mean
    Missing strategy: 
    Coverage (how often a stored strategy existed) is reported separately for preflop, flop, turn and river, heads-up and multiway, and stack depth. High preflop coverage does not mean postflop coverage is high.
    Accuracy: exact distance from equilibrium is measured only for small reference games (reports/0.30-math-validation.json). Full-game accuracy is not proven.
    

    

    Strategy quality
    

    How close each stored strategy is to an equilibrium for its own small game (the board, pot, stacks, opponent ranges and bet sizes it was trained for). Finished training is not proof of accuracy. Rivers can be checked exactly; flop and turn strategies stay Unverified because their later streets are not stored. None of this measures the full eight-player tournament game.

    What the statuses mean
    Validated: an exact check of this specific game met the target. Estimated: a sampled check with a stated uncertainty. Restricted check: checked only against limited responses or an approximation. Unverified: not enough evidence; usable only in labelled simulation experiments. Failed quality check: worse than the target; refused for decisions.
    Heads-up strategies are measured by exploitability (how much a perfect opponent could win). Strategies with three or more players are measured by NashConv, a different number: a low NashConv does not make a strategy safe against several opponents working together.
    

    Opponent ranges
    

    Which hands each player could hold, worked out from their betting and the stored strategies. These are estimates under stated assumptions, never knowledge of anybody's hidden cards.
