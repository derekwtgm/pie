# EightMax Windows 5.16

Build `39600509cc5a98e8`. Successor of 5.15 (`87b9f6e38b735cfb`). This is **Phase 6** of the ten-phase plan: card-grouping
error.

**The short version.** 5.16 is mostly a measurement. The same eight small games were solved with the three hand
groupings of the Multiway form (Compact, Detailed, Exact cards), and every result was scored the same way: the exact
NashConv of the strategy in the game with exact cards, where the responder sees every card. Rules for reading the
numbers were written first (`validation/5.16/plan.json`).

* **Compact has a large error.** large on 6 of 8 games (`rg-hu-river`, `rg-river3`, `rg-hu-turn`, `rg-turn3`, `rg-turn4`, `rg-flop3c`); 0.11 to 17.42 percent of the pot a player after 10,000,000 deals, 2.2 to 108 times Exact cards; settled on 7 games, still falling on 1. More training does not remove it: it is the same after 200,000 and
  after 10,000,000 deals.
* **Detailed does not.** large on 0 of 8 games; 0.00 to 5.00 percent of the pot a player after 10,000,000 deals, 0.6 to 3 times Exact cards; settled on 2 games, still falling on 6. On these games it is as good as Exact cards and often better at equal
  time, because it needs fewer rows.
* **Because Compact's error is large, a better grouping was to be proposed.** The proposal is **Ranked**
  (`ranked-5.16-v1`), a new choice in the form. It beats Compact on all eight games and on two held-out games and
  keeps its table at about 20% of Detailed's on the large fixture. **It is not as good as Detailed**:
  large on 2 of 8 games (`rg-hu-turn`, `rg-flop3c`); 0.01 to 12.99 percent of the pot a player after 10,000,000 deals, 1.3 to 9 times Exact cards; settled on 7 games, still falling on 1. It is offered for studies where Detailed does not fit in memory, which is where Compact is used
  today. The first proposal failed its size check and was dropped (CONVERGENCE-5.16.md, section 6).

What this does not tell you: anything about games with hundreds of hands a seat (the shipped fixtures) or about
preflop studies. No exact best response exists there, so the error of a grouping cannot be measured there with these
tools. The games measured have 10 to 60 hands a seat, which is kind to every grouping.

No trainer changed, no default changed, and Compact, Detailed and Exact cards write the states of 5.15.

First thing to do on your PC: **WINDOWS-TESTS-5.16.md**. Reports: FINAL-REPORT-5.16.md, CONVERGENCE-5.16.md (the
measurement), MATHEMATICAL-VALIDATION-5.16.md, VALIDATION-5.16.md, BUILD-5.16.md.

## What is new

### 1. The measurement (nothing to install or switch on)

`validation/5.16/`: the plan with its five amendments, the script that runs a unit (`grouping516.py`), the analysis
(`analyze516.py`), every unit's result, and the summaries. CONVERGENCE-5.16.md is the report. The games are
generated by `source/tools/games516.js`; between the versions of a game only the grouping differs.

### 2. The Ranked grouping

*Multiway > Hand grouping > Ranked (5.16)*, for studies that start on the flop, turn or river with sampled deals.

A hand's row is chosen by three things:

* **where its strength stands inside the range of its own seat** on the current board: the position of its hand
  value among the distinct hand values of that range, in at most 16 levels (exact when the range has at most 16
  distinct strengths on that board);
* **a class of each new board card**: it pairs the board, is higher than every board card, is a ten or higher, or
  is lower; and whether it is the third card of its suit;
* **the two draws of Compact**: four to a flush, four to a straight.

Compact asks "what kind of hand is this" on a fixed scale (pair, two pair, ..., with the top rank in steps of three).
In a range full of king-high hands that scale puts most of the range into two or three groups. Ranked asks "where is
this hand in my range", so the kicker that decides between two king-high hands is kept.

What Ranked loses compared with Detailed: the exact rank of the new card, blockers, the texture of the board, and,
in ranges with more than 16 distinct strengths, the exact order of hands. The numbers are in CONVERGENCE-5.16.md.

From the command line: `"abstraction":"ranked"` and `"groupKeys":{"abstraction":"ranked",...}` in the spec, as for
Compact.

### 3. `mwcli --selftest516` and the Windows validation of 5.16

`mwcli.exe --selftest516 studies <work-dir>`: 6 checks (8 recorded states of Ranked studies, threads, stop and
continue, what the trainer reports; checkpoint rules and refusals). `validate_windows516.ps1` is the 5.15 script
with two more sections (`self-test 5.16`, `grouping 5.16`).

## What did not change

* **Every trainer, every default, and the three existing groupings.** The unchanged 5.15 program and 5.16 train
  225 configurations and 224 fixture cases, 0 differences to the same bytes; the self-tests of 5.7 to 5.15 pass with their recorded tables untouched. The
  default grouping of the form is still Detailed.
* **Checkpoints.** A state trained with Ranked names `"grouping":"ranked-5.16-v1"` in its header and writes
  its row format as `"rowKeys":2` (Compact and Detailed states keep `"rowKeys":1` and the headers of 5.15). It continues only
  under a Ranked study. Studies with another grouping refuse it, and the programs of 5.10 to 5.15 refuse the Ranked
  spec and state with a reason and write nothing (tested with the programs of 5.15, 5.14, 5.13, 5.12, 5.11, 5.10). The second header value exists
  because of a fault the final checks found in the first frozen build (`3490f9edf48ec1db`): the earlier programs ignored
  the new field and continued a Ranked state under the Compact study of the same spot. That build was withdrawn, the
  row-format value was added, and every measurement and check of this release was repeated on the released build.
  Every other state and checkpoint loads and continues as before. The layout of the rows did not change.
* **The fixtures.** They keep their groupings (most use Compact). 5.16 does not change them: their recorded results
  belong to their grouping, and whether Ranked is better at their size is not measured.
